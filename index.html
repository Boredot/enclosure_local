Rules
  • Draw from one of your nodes to any point within a ±3 box in x and y.
  • Up to 2 lines per turn (Blue gets only 1 on the very first turn).
  • Crossing a vulnerable enemy line cuts it (max 1 cut per line). Orphaned enemy nodes are removed.
  • Lines you place this turn are invincible until your turn ends.
  • You may not overlap your own lines, put nodes on your own edges, or run edges through your own nodes.
  • At the end of every turn, each player's enclosed area is added to their score.
  • After 120 edge-slots are used, the higher score wins.
